## Note

This article is currently available in Chinese. It reflects on how to make a personal presentation deck for scholarship or honor-defense scenarios, using my Cheng Siwei Honorary President Scholarship defense as the main example.

The web version highlights the main reading path, while the original PDF preserves the full layout, examples, and detailed discussion.

## Main Ideas

The article treats PPT not as decoration, but as a way to organize information. A strong personal presentation should help the audience quickly understand who you are, what you have done, how those experiences connect, and where they point next.

The workflow is organized around three stages: outline, layout, and design. The outline determines whether the story works; the layout determines whether the audience can read it; the design determines whether the story is memorable.
