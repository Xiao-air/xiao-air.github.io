## Context

As projects and learning materials grow, simply collecting information makes it harder to reuse what matters.

## Actions

Reorganized materials by theme, problem, and project node, then tagged each experience with context, evidence, and follow-up actions.

## Results

The knowledge base shifted from a storage folder into a working system that supports resumes, reflection, and planning.

## Growth

Learned that good knowledge management is not collecting more; it is making important experience reusable when needed.
