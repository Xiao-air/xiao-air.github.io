## Context

Repetitive work consumes attention and often traps know-how inside personal memory instead of reusable systems.

## Actions

Mapped frequent tasks, identified standard inputs and checks, and gradually turned them into repeatable workflows.

## Results

The work became more stable, easier to hand off, and more useful as a foundation for a personal operating system.

## Growth

Learned to use process design to reduce cognitive load while keeping human judgment at the center.
