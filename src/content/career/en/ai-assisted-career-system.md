## Context

Career planning needs continuous updates instead of being reconstructed only during job searches or annual reviews.

## Actions

Use the public site for selected career timeline stories and keep complete retrospectives, sensitive details, and concrete action plans in the private vault.

## Results

Created a foundation that separates public presentation from private self-management, while making every future project node reusable.

## Growth

Treat AI as a companion for organizing and reflection, not as a replacement for personal judgment.
