  This project builds a Data-ML-DFT digital teaching workflow for Cu-Zn catalyst research on CO2 hydrogenation to methanol.

  It then connects machine-learning screening with DFT candidate validation, exploring how AI4S methods can be translated into catalysis teaching and research.
