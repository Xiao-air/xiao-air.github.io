## Context

Cross-functional work can slow down when goals are interpreted differently, information is out of sync, or ownership is unclear.

## Actions

Broke projects into stage goals, key risks, collaboration interfaces, and acceptance criteria, then maintained a regular sync rhythm.

## Results

Project progress became easier to see, and collaborators could quickly understand current status, next steps, and support needed.

## Growth

Learned that project driving is not just pushing speed; it is the work of continuously reducing uncertainty.
