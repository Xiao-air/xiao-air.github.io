## Context

Early career experiences often lived as scattered tasks and outcomes, making it hard to review what capabilities were actually growing.

## Actions

Structured each experience into context, actions, results, and growth, while separating public evidence from private reflection.

## Results

Built a sustainable career timeline structure for both the public resume extension site and the private reflection vault.

## Growth

Learned that career growth is not only a list of outcomes, but also judgment, collaboration, reflection, and long-term direction.
