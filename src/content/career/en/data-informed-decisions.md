## Context

Many work decisions rely on experience and intuition, but they are hard to explain without evidence.

## Actions

Defined key metrics around goals, organized available data, and presented findings with concise views and recommendations.

## Results

Decision discussions became more focused, and retrospectives could better separate facts, judgments, and assumptions.

## Growth

Built the habit of using data to serve the question instead of stacking charts for their own sake.
