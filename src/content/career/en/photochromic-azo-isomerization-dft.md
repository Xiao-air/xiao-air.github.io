title: 'Theoretical Study of Z/E Isomerization and Photophysical Properties of Photochromic Azo Materials'
period: '2026.08 - Present'
summary: |-
  This project studies Z/E photoisomerization in five photochromic azo materials through theoretical computation.
  Geometry optimization, reaction-path scans, transition-state searches, and frequency/IRC validation are used to compare rotation and inversion pathways.
  TD-DFT, frontier molecular orbital analysis, and absorption spectra are combined to discuss substituent effects on photoresponse and molecular design.
