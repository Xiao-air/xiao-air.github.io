title: 'Assembly and Regulation of Supramolecular Hydrogels'
period: '2023.10 - 2025.05'
summary: |-
  This project explored supramolecular hydrogels and bioresponsive soft materials through hydrogel motifs designed for esterase-related liver cancer cell environments.
  Characterization of gelation behavior, microstructure, and biological evaluation helped connect molecular design with the assembly process and structure-property relationship.
  The project focused on selectivity, biocompatibility, and spatially controlled self-assembly, providing experimental support for future responsive soft-material design.
