title: 'Process Improvement for Sodium-Ion Cathode Precursor Materials'
period: '2024.10 - 2025.05'
summary: |-
  This project focused on improving the coprecipitation process for Ni-Fe-Mn sodium-ion cathode precursor materials while reducing wastewater pressure.
  Literature, patent, and industry research were used to identify key process variables, followed by optimization around pH, stirring, aging, and complexation.
  A resource-recovery route for production wastewater was designed alongside the materials process, connecting precursor synthesis with by-product utilization.
